Reading guide:
  Demo A: no torque -> gravity is unopposed -> falls in <0.5 s
  Demo B/C: constant knee torque -> small pre-load on the spring-like
            joints -> falls a bit later, but no FEEDBACK to recover
            from disturbances. Open-loop control cannot balance.
